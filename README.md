## About the Report

This report studies the functioning of statistical departments at the **Central and State Government levels**.

### Central Government
- Collection and compilation of national statistics
- Population, economic and social data analysis
- Conducting large-scale surveys
- Preparation of statistical reports and indicators
- Supporting government planning and policy-making

### State Government
- Collection of state-level statistical data
- Preparation of economic and social statistics
- District-level data analysis
- Supporting state planning and development programmes
- Coordination with central statistical agencies

## Objectives

- Understand the structure and functions of government statistical departments.
- Study methods of data collection, processing and analysis.
- Examine the role of statistics in planning and decision-making.
- Understand the importance of reliable official statistics.

## Conclusion

Government statistical departments provide reliable data that supports **planning, policy formulation, resource allocation and evaluation of development programmes** at both Central and State levels.
